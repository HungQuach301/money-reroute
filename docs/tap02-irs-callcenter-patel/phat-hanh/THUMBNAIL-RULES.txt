The thumbnail MUST NOT contain:
1. Any real person's face, body, or likeness. No portraits, no photos, no "inspired by" faces. No recognizable older person.
2. Any recognizable face at all. If a human appears, show only hands, a silhouette, or a figure from behind, out of focus.
3. Logos, seals, badges, wordmarks, colors, letterheads, or interface screens of the IRS, USCIS, DHS, any police or tax agency (including Canada and Australia), any bank, card brand, money-transfer service, or court. The word "IRS" may appear only as plain text next to the word "FAKE".
4. Any phone number, real or invented, and any calling or messaging app screen. Phone screens must be blank or glowing white.
5. The words "SCAM", "SCAMMER", "FRAUDSTER", "CON MAN", "CRIMINAL", or "LIAR" attached to a person, and the name of any call center.
6. Anything that looks like a real court document, government letter, tax form, check, gift card, or official seal (no fake signatures, case numbers, or letterheads).
7. Stereotyped images of India or of any nationality, religion, or ethnic group: no religious symbols, traditional clothing, flags, or street scenes. A plain flat map line is allowed.
8. Text that promises something the video does not show (no "SECRET", "EXPOSED", "THEY DON'T WANT YOU TO KNOW").
9. Blood, weapons, handcuffs on a person, mugshot styling, or shocking imagery.

The thumbnail MAY contain:
- The words "FAKE IRS CALLS" (the callers impersonated the agency; the IRS had nothing to do with the calls).
- Abstract props: a phone with a blank glowing screen, plain blank white plastic cards, an arrow, a dotted line across a flat world map.
- Channel look (same as episode 1): warm ivory paper background, dark ink, one accent color; bold serif headline, max 4 words.

Format: 1280x720, high contrast, readable at small size.
