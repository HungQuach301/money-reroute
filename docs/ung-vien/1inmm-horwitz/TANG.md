## Cách nói EN gợi ý (đúng tầng)
- **Horwitz (●, hình sự):** "In October 2021, Zachary Horwitz pleaded guilty in federal court in Los Angeles to one count of securities fraud. In February 2022, a federal judge sentenced him to 20 years in prison and ordered him to pay about $230 million in restitution." · Kể chi tiết sơ đồ: "In his plea agreement, Horwitz admitted that…" (chỉ dùng phần Factual Basis N7 tr. 8–12). · **Không** nói "convicted of wire fraud / identity theft" (Counts 2–13 bị bác). · Có thể nói "Prosecutors had also charged additional securities fraud counts, wire fraud and aggravated identity theft; those counts were dismissed under the plea deal" (N8/N9 mục 75; N6).
- **Horwitz (◐, dân sự SEC):** "The SEC also sued him. In 2024 he consented to a final judgment, without a trial, that bars him from violating the antifraud laws; the roughly $74 million he owed in that case was deemed satisfied by the criminal restitution order." · **Chưa dùng** "without admitting or denying": bản Consent chưa mở được, và §V của judgment coi các cáo buộc là đã thừa nhận cho mục đích phá sản. · Không bao giờ nói "found liable" cho vụ này.
- **1inMM Capital (◐):** "The SEC alleged that Horwitz and his company, 1inMM Capital…" · Hoặc gắn với lời nhận tội: "Horwitz admitted that his company, 1inMM Capital, generally did not acquire the film rights…" (sửa theo KIEM-TANG: N7 tr. 10 ghi "generally")
- **HBO / Netflix:** "HBO and Netflix were not accused of any wrongdoing; according to the SEC, neither company ever did business with 1inMM." · Nhắc tên chỉ ở dạng chữ, không logo (HIEN-CHUONG §4).
- **Nạn nhân:** "investors, many of them his personal friends" (N12); "five major investor groups and more than 250 sub-investors" (N7). Không nêu tên.
