## Cách nói EN gợi ý
- **Shan Hanes (hình sự):** "In May 2024, Shan Hanes pleaded guilty in federal court in Kansas to one count of embezzlement by a bank officer. In August 2024, a federal judge in Wichita sentenced him to 293 months — about 24 years — in prison." Có thể dùng các sự việc ông **thừa nhận trong plea agreement**: "In his plea agreement, Hanes admitted he lied to bank employees and board members about the purpose of the transfers." Nguồn trên hình: "Source: United States v. Hanes, No. 6:24-cr-10013 (D. Kan. 2024)".
- **Không** nói "he stole for himself" như sự thật đã kết luận ngoài hồ sơ; dùng đúng từ hồ sơ "embezzled … for the purchase of cryptocurrency intended for the personal benefit of the defendant" (N1 tr. 3). Lời "he was also a victim" chỉ nói là lập luận của bên bào chữa: "His lawyer argued he had fallen victim to a 'pig butchering' scam."
- **Fed ban:** "The Federal Reserve Board later barred him from the banking industry in an order he agreed to without admitting or denying the Board's allegations."
- **Kẻ lừa đảo:** "Prosecutors said the money ended up in crypto accounts controlled by unidentified third parties. As of the latest filings we reviewed, no one else has been charged." (Kiểm lại trước khi đăng.)
- **Ngân hàng / MLR:** "A Federal Reserve inspector general review found that bank staff approved wires far above the bank's own daily limits." (MLR viết 02/2024 dùng chữ "alleged" cho Hanes. Chỉ trích MLR về kiểm soát, không trích như kết luận tội.)
- **Vụ bang:** không nhắc, hoặc chỉ nói "He also faced state charges in Kansas" kèm "status not verified". Khuyến nghị bỏ.
