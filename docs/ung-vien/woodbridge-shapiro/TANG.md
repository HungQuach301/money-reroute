## Cách nói EN gợi ý
- **Robert Shapiro (hình sự):** "In August 2019, Robert Shapiro pleaded guilty in federal court in Miami to conspiracy to commit mail and wire fraud and to tax evasion. In October 2019, a federal judge sentenced him to 25 years in prison — the maximum for those two counts." Trên hình: "Source: United States v. Shapiro, No. 1:19-cr-20178 (S.D. Fla. 2019)". Sự việc ông **thừa nhận** trong factual proffer được nói thẳng nhưng ghi nguồn: "In his plea, Shapiro admitted that new investors' money was used to pay 'returns' to earlier investors." Nếu nhắc đơn giảm án: "In 2024, a federal appeals court upheld the denial of his request for early release."
- **Shapiro / Woodbridge (SEC):** "Shapiro and the Woodbridge companies settled the SEC's civil case without admitting or denying the allegations. The court ordered Shapiro to pay a $100 million civil penalty." Đơn kiện 2017 luôn là "The SEC alleged…", vd "The SEC alleged that most of the so-called third-party borrowers were actually companies Shapiro owned."
- **Woodbridge (công ty):** không nói "Woodbridge was convicted"; công ty không bị truy tố hình sự. Nói "the company filed for bankruptcy in December 2017" và "settled the SEC case without admitting or denying".
- **Ivan Acevedo:** "Ivan Acevedo, a former Woodbridge director of investments, pleaded guilty to a fraud conspiracy charge and was sentenced in 2021 to five years in prison." Source: "United States v. Acevedo, No. 1:19-cr-20178 (S.D. Fla. 2021)".
- **Dane Roseman:** "Court records show Dane Roseman, who succeeded Acevedo, was also convicted and sentenced." (sửa theo KIEM-TANG: "pleaded guilty" chưa kiểm được trên tài liệu đã mở — N15 tr. 6 chỉ nói "criminal conviction and sentence") **Không** nói mức án cho tới khi mở được judgment Doc. 324 (hoặc thông cáo DOJ N22).
- **Đại lý bán hàng:** khuyến nghị không nêu tên; nói chung "The SEC separately sued a number of sales agents, alleging they sold Woodbridge notes without being registered as brokers."
- **Vợ Shapiro / relief defendants:** không nêu tên.
